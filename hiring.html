Straight answers

What roofers ask before they ring

Will you contact my current employer?

No. Not unless and until you tell us to, and never before you have accepted something. Everyone who applies is currently working somewhere and we are not going to make that awkward for you.

I have not got a CV. Does that matter?

Not at all. We are not going to learn anything about whether you can lay a roof from a Word document. Leave a number and we will have a conversation instead.

I do pitched roofing, not flat. Should I still ring?

Yes. If you have roofing experience and you want to move into flat systems, say so on the call. Plenty of good flat roofers started somewhere else.

I am an improver rather than a time-served roofer.

Still ring. We are hiring improvers as well, and we will put you alongside somebody who explains what they are doing rather than leaving you to guess.

Which areas are you hiring in?

Kettering and Northamptonshire, Birmingham and the West Midlands, and London. Tell us where you are and we will tell you what is running near you.

How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to ring the same day, and we would rather ring you and say "not right now" than leave you wondering. Nobody who applies gets ignored.
