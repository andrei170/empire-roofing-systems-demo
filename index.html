Straight answers

What building owners ask us

Can my flat roof be refurbished, or does it need replacing?

It depends whether the existing build-up has perished. If it has not, refurbishment is usually faster, cheaper and less disruptive. A survey answers it properly — and we will tell you when replacement genuinely is the better value option rather than selling you the bigger job by default.

How long is the guarantee?

Between 10 and 30 years depending on the system specified and the manufacturer behind it. The guarantee is part of the specification conversation, not an afterthought — and most manufacturer warranties expect the roof to be maintained, which is why we offer maintenance packages.

What does NFRC membership actually mean?

The National Federation of Roofing Contractors is the trade body for UK roofing. Membership means a firm is vetted against the federation's standards. For commercial specifiers it is often a requirement on the tender; for everyone else it is a reasonable shorthand for "this firm is accountable to somebody".

Do you do domestic work as well as commercial?

Yes. Extensions, garages, dormers and outbuildings. Commercial and industrial refurbishment is the specialism, but the same crews and the same standards apply to a garage roof.

What is a flame-free system and do I need one?

It is a felt system installed without a torch. It is used where hot works are restricted — occupied buildings, timber structures, sensitive sites, or where an insurer requires it. If that applies to your building we will say so at survey.

Can you work around our operation?

Usually yes, and on commercial sites it is normally the whole point. Phasing, out-of-hours sections and staged handovers are all normal on occupied buildings.
